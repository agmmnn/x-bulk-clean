# Privacy

X Bulk Clean does not collect, sell, or transmit personal data to the project's developers.

The extension reads and interacts with pages on `x.com` and `twitter.com` only when needed to perform an action selected by the user. Preferences (such as theme, speed, and performance mode), your never-unfollow whitelist, job history, affected-record details, and CSV backup data are stored locally through Chrome's extension storage until you remove individual jobs or use **Clear local data** in Settings. CSV exports are created locally and downloaded by the browser.

Preview scan results remain locally in the active X tab, side panel, and Chrome's in-memory session storage (`chrome.storage.session`) while the browser session is active. They are never transmitted to the project's developers or an external service.

The extension has no analytics, advertising, payment processing, license validation, telemetry, or project-operated backend. X itself may process activity performed on its website under its own privacy policy.

If a future contribution introduces data collection or a new external service, this document and the extension permissions must be updated before release.
